We are a Green focused, 501c3 nonprofit, no kill animal rescue established in 2006. Our mission is to rescue, rehabilitate and re-home abused and neglected animals. We are dedicated to the welfare of these animals and the gentle ecosystem that we share. We rescue all breeds, including pit bulls, and focus our&nbsp;efforts on larger, elderly, sick or injured animals in need of extra care. Our rescued pets are placed into loving foster homes and receive one on one attention, complete vet care, training and socialization. We also educate the public on humane and responsible pet ownership through nonprofit partnerships, public education programs and grass root efforts within communities.
