<p>ICAN was founded in 2007 and is a volunteer run, no-cage, no-kill, animal shelter in beautiful British Columbia located in Invermere, BC. ICAN main focus is felines, from 1 day old to adult cats od all sages and health status.&nbsp; &nbsp;ICAN will take dogs and small animals if foster homes permit.&nbsp;&nbsp; We are a registered non-profit charity and have been working out of a 110 year old house for the past several years and are rapidly outgrowing this location.&nbsp; &nbsp;Despite this, we have assisted more than 2,000 companion animals to date and the numbers grow largeer every year.</p>

<p>Please visit our website at www.icanbc.com or like us on Facebook to learn more about ICAN and to see our amazing animals for adoption!&nbsp;</p>
