<p>DFW Pug Rescue Club, Inc. is a non-profit corporation with Federal Tax Exemption as a public charity under Section 501 (c) 3 of the Internal Revenue Code.&nbsp; Founded in 1996, DFWPRC is one of the largest pug rescue organizations in the United States and has rescued and placed thousands of adorable pugs.</p>

<p>All pugs rescued by DFWPRC, are taken immediately to the vet where they are examined, vaccinated, sterilized, receive rabies shots, dental care (if needed), tested, and treated for heartworms. All are completed before being placed in a foster or permanent home.</p>

<p>Approximately thirty-five percent (35%) of the rescue pugs are heartworm positive and all are treated at DFWPRC&rsquo;s expense. We are an all-volunteer, non-profit organization and do not have a staging area to hold our rescue pugs. We depend on the generosity of our volunteers to provide foster homes until permanent homes can be located. We rescue more males than females (10 to 1), more fawns than blacks (40 to 1), and the average age of our rescue pugs is 4 years old. The average life span of a pug is between 14 &ndash; 17 years.</p>
