<p>The Central New York SPCA mission is to advocate on behalf of all of the animals who come to our shelter. &quot;We speak for those who cannot speak for themselves.&quot;</p>

<p>Since its formation in 1891, the Central New York SPCA has dealt with the issues of animal neglect and cruelty.&nbsp;Our founders began this organization because of the mistreatment of the mules pulling the barges on the Erie Canal.&nbsp;&nbsp;</p>

<p><strong>Our Mission is:</strong></p>

<ul>
	<li>Investigation, intervention and prosecution of animal abuse and neglect</li>
	<li>Education of the public to the value and rewards of responsible guardianship of companion animals and respect for all living creatures</li>
	<li>Sterilization of all companion animals to reduce the tragic over-population of unwanted animals through programs we design and propose.</li>
	<li>Legislation to provide for maximum protection of animals and severe penalties for those who abuse them.</li>
	<li>Creation and maintenance of excellent living conditions, compassionate care and medical care for the thousands of animals in our care every year.</li>
	<li>Adoption of those animals into responsible homes where they will be given the precious gift of a lifetime of love.</li>
	<li>We handle animal abuse and neglect for parts of Onondaga County, all of Madison County and all of Oneida County</li>
</ul>
