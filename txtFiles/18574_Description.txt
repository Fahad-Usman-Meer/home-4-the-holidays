<p>The City of Great Falls Animal Shelter (GFAS) is an Open Admission Municipal animal shelter serving the citizens and animals of the City of Great Falls.</p>

<p>The Mission of the Shelter is to&nbsp;<em>ensure the health, safety and welfare of the animals and citizens of our community through education about responsible pet ownership</em>.</p>

<p>We use many resources to&nbsp;re-home every adoptable animal through adoption, rescue partners, and adoption events. By utilizing these resources and with the help of the Great Falls Community we do not euthanize animals&nbsp;due to space or length of stay at the City of Great Falls Animal Shelter.&nbsp;</p>
