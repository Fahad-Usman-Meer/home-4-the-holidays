<h1>C.A.R.E., Community Animal Rescue Effort, is a foster based organization.&nbsp;</h1>

<h2>While our PO Box is in Columbus, Indiana, CARE has no buildings - We are also 100% volunteer based from our board members, our fosters, and our volunteers. We donate our time, our homes, and quite often our cleaning supplies.&nbsp; All of our adoption fees, fundraised money, grants, and donations goes toward caring for the animals until the day they are adopted. The money donated to CARE&nbsp; goes to vet expenses (and we take in a lot of medical cases, broken limb repair, amputations, respiratory problems, parasite issues, fleas, heartworm treatment, etc.), food, litter, puppy supplies, kennels, crates, and a bare minimum of promotional material/other supplies.</h2>

<h2>We use sponsorships and our local support systems to provide toys, collars, leashes, and treats.&nbsp;</h2>
